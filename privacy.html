Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information Hawke Tech Solutions (ABN 84429688089) collects and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile number and business name, when you fill in our contact form.
  • Anything you tell us when you call, text, email or meet with us.
  • Information about how the site is used, such as pages viewed, device type, general location and how you arrived.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to you, prepare a website preview if you ask for one, provide the services you buy, and keep records of our dealings with you. We use usage information to understand and improve our websites.

Who handles it

Form submissions are stored in Google Sheets and emailed to us using Google services. Website usage is measured with Google Analytics. These services may store information outside Australia. We don't sell your information.

Website previews

We sometimes prepare a private preview of a website for a business using publicly available information. Preview links aren't listed on search engines, and we record when a link is opened so we know whether to follow up. If you'd like a preview about your business removed, contact us and we'll delete it.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing jesse@hawketech.com.au or calling 0410 640 220. If you have a privacy complaint, contact us first. If you aren't satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
